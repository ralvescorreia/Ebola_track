Ebola real dataset 2013-2026
Columns: Country, Region, Report_Date, Age_Group, New_Cases, New_Deaths

Important: Age_Group is "All ages" because public outbreak summaries do not provide consistent age-stratified data across all Ebola outbreaks from 2013 to 2026.
The 2013 row is the retrospective WHO-identified first West Africa case in Meliandou, Guinea, who fell ill on 26 Dec 2013 and died two days later.
West Africa rows through 2015-01-05 are computed as differences between cumulative WHO situation-report values from cmrivers/ebola country_timeseries.csv. Negative deltas are retained and represent reporting corrections/revisions in cumulative bulletins, not negative infections.
Rows from 2016 onward are outbreak-level totals using CDC/WHO public outbreak summaries; Report_Date is the end/declaration/current report date.
